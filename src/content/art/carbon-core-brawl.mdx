<Heading level={2} size="4xl">Project Overview</Heading>

These characters were created for Carbon Core: Brawl, a multiplayer demo developed at Qualcomm and showcased at CES. Balor was completed as a fully playable character and integrated into the final experience, while Mizetto was developed during production but ultimately did not make it into the demo due to time and integration constraints.
