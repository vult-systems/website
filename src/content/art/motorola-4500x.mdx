<Heading level={2} size="4xl">Project Overview</Heading>

A recreation of the Motorola M4500X car phone created as the first project in my prop art course. Developed entirely through traditional box modeling techniques without the use of ZBrush, this project served as an introduction to game-ready prop creation, focusing on clean topology, UVs, texturing, and real-time presentation.
