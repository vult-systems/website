<Heading level={2} size="4xl">Project Overview</Heading>

Aurech is the protagonist of an upcoming character vignette for *Monomyth*, an original IP created by [Randy Bishop](https://www.randybishopart.com/). I developed the character's production topology to support the rig created by [Balazs Banyai](https://balazsbanyai.artstation.com/), with a focus on clean, anatomy-driven deformation and the specific shapes Randy needed Aurech to achieve in animation.

The turntables below showcase the clay and wireframe passes, the hair model developed with grooming in mind, and the additional detail work completed for the teeth.
