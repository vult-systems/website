<Heading level={2} size="4xl">Project Overview</Heading>
RD-10 is a real-time character created for the ArtStation *Beneath the Waves* challenge and completed over the course of a month. Like many personal projects, this was something I worked on whenever I could find time outside of work. I'm happy with where the project landed, but more importantly, I learned a tremendous amount throughout the process, met many incredibly talented artists, and had a great time participating in the challenge. If you've never joined one of these community events, I highly recommend giving one a shot.
