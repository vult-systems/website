<Heading level={2} size="4xl">Project Overview</Heading>

This character was initially created for a real-time cinematic showcasing Unity HDRP on Qualcomm Snapdragon devices. As production evolved, the team pivoted toward a multi-platform technical demo designed to showcase the full capabilities of the hardware.

The final project included a real-time cinematic in Unity HDRP, a third-person over-the-shoulder shooter in UE4, and an AR drone experience, all connected through a shared multiplayer lobby for players to access and explore.

## Role & Responsibilities

I was responsible for all aspects of character development, including:

- **Hi-Poly** - Initial cloth blockout in Marvelous Designer for simulation, followed by detailing, cleanup, and hard-surface refinement in ZBrush
- **Optimization & Retopology** - Game-ready meshes and multiple LODs created in Maya for each platform experience and player controller
- **Texturing** - Physically based materials authored in Substance Painter to meet platform-specific requirements
- **Engine Integration** - Character implementation in both Unity HDRP and Unreal Engine 4
- **Lighting & Presentation** - Final look development and presentation renders to support visual fidelity and marketing goals
