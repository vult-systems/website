## Character Concept

A female variant of the Foundation Explorer series, designed with the same modular tactical approach as the original. This character maintains the utilitarian aesthetic while exploring different silhouette proportions and gear configurations.

## Design Approach

Building on the Foundation Explorer design language:
- Modular equipment systems
- Functional tactical gear placement
- Balanced proportions and readable silhouette
- Consistent material and surface treatment

## Technical Details

Character modeling workflow:
- Base mesh and proportions established in Maya
- High-resolution sculpting in ZBrush
- Hard-surface detailing for tactical equipment
- Modular attachment system for gear customization

## Series Continuity

This variant explores how the Foundation Explorer design system adapts across different character types while maintaining visual consistency and functional design principles.
