## Project Overview

Contributed to character development for BioWare's Star Wars: The Old Republic expansion content. Focused on creating high-fidelity character assets for cinematic sequences and in-game hero characters, working within the established Star Wars visual language while pushing the quality bar for the MMO space.

<Image src={valkorian01} alt="Valkorian Character Art" class="rounded-md border border-border my-8" />

## Technical Details

### Character Sculpting
- **Software**: ZBrush for high-poly sculpting
- **Style**: Realistic proportions with stylized details matching Star Wars aesthetic
- **Detail Level**: Cinematic-quality assets for key story moments

<Image src={valkorian02} alt="Valkorian Detail View" class="rounded-md border border-border my-8" />

### Texturing & Materials
- **Pipeline**: PBR workflow optimized for real-time cinematics
- **Maps**: Full PBR texture sets with subsurface scattering for skin
- **Resolution**: 4K hero textures with optimized game-ready versions

<Image src={valkorian03} alt="Valkorian Alternate Angle" class="rounded-md border border-border my-8" />

### Pipeline Integration
- **Engine**: Integrated with BioWare's proprietary engine
- **Optimization**: LOD chain for performance across hardware tiers
- **Collaboration**: Worked closely with animation and lighting teams
