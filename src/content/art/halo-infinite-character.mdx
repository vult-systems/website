<Heading level={2} size="4xl">Project Overview</Heading>

As part of the Game Model Shop (GMS) Armor Team on Halo Infinite: Cyber Showdown III, I created the Grimghast armor set for the Chimera Spartan Core. The process began with a ZBrush blockout to establish the 3D translation of the concept art for approval, followed by final production modeling in Maya using 343 Industries’ internal crease-set SubD workflow.
